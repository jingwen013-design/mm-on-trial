\section{Reproducibility}\label{app:D}

The code, data and \LaTeX{} source of this paper are at \url{https://github.com/jingwen013-design/mm-on-trial} (release v1.0, 19 October 2026). The notebook mm\_on\_trial\_report.ipynb follows the order of the paper. By default it loads the cached results of exp1.py (Section~\ref{sec:mkt}), tables.py (Tables~\ref{tab:tcja} and \ref{tab:C1}--\ref{tab:C3}), calib2.py (Section~\ref{sec:calib}), extras.py (numbers quoted in the text that no table shows) and the figures from figs.py and fig\_firms.py (with style.py), and reruns them all when RECOMPUTE is set. model.py holds the model and firms.json the firm data. Two-state results and the multi-period results of Section~\ref{sec:multi} are exact; the other lognormal and normal results use fixed random seeds with 150,000--400,000 draws, so that reruns reproduce every reported figure. Market-priced debt is solved by root-finding on the lenders' break-even condition. As an independent check, independent\_check.py, which does not use model.py, values the lognormal case by numerical integration and reproduces $\VU = 65.45$, $\Dstar = 44.44$, Table~\ref{tab:twostate} and the lognormal optimum ($D \approx 33.8$, gain 0.557, default probability 0.92\%).
