\section{Firm-level detail}\label{app:E}

\textit{Singapore Airlines' 2020--21 financing.} The S\$8.8 billion raised in 2020 combined a rights issue priced 31.8\% below the theoretical ex-rights price with mandatory convertible bonds, backstopped by Temasek; SIA also arranged a S\$4 billion bridge loan and in 2021 issued a further S\$6.2 billion of mandatory convertible bonds \citep{sia2020}.
